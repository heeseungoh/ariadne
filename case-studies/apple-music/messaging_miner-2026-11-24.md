# What users love (2026-11-24)

**Question:** What do happy users value, in their own words?

**Method:** 1645 4-5 star App Store reviews, each given one theme from a fixed list of 13 (pack `messaging_miner-v1`), labelled by keyword rules, with no AI. The rules only match words, so expect some mislabelled reviews and a larger 'other' group than with the Claude labeller. Quotes are the sentence that matched. An app needs 30 reviews for percentages and 10 for theme counts; below that its reviews are listed without themes.

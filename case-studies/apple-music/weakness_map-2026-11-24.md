# Competitor weakness map (2026-11-24)

**Question:** Where is each competitor losing users?

**Method:** 580 1-3 star App Store reviews, each given one theme from a fixed list of 14 (pack `weakness_map-v3`), labelled by keyword rules, with no AI. The rules only match words, so expect some mislabelled reviews and a larger 'other' group than with the Claude labeller. Quotes are the sentence that matched. An app needs 30 reviews for percentages and 10 for theme counts; below that its reviews are listed without themes.
